What "mix" means

A page has parts. A mix takes, part by part, the idea that does that part best, and says where each came from. So every option below differs in each part, and the mix picks per row:

Option E: Pots: every goal a jar that fills

Like savings pots and vaults in banking apps: each goal is a glass jar that fills from the bottom, with what's left in big type. The fastest thing to read on a phone; a goal you can't see the money for shows a locked jar.

Option G: Forecast: where this pace lands

Like trend charts in analytics and health apps: what you've saved month by month, and a dotted line to where that pace reaches the target. Answers "will we make it?" in one sentence. Needs goal history (new: a monthly snapshot).

Beach trip: at this pace you'll be there in March, about 3 months early.

You've added about $320 a month since May. June needs $200 a month.

Solid: what you've saved. Dotted: where this pace goes. Needs a little history: a goal shows its forecast after its second month.

Option H: This month: a checklist of set-asides

Like to-do lists and instalment plans: the month as a short list of "put aside $200 for Beach trip", ticked as money lands. Turns goals into one thing to do this month. Needs each account's balance on the 1st (new: a monthly snapshot).
