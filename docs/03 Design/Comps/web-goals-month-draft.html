The "By" month: options

Today it is the browser's own month box: dashes, a tiny icon, and a different popup in every browser (Safari on iPhone shows a wheel, Chrome a calendar of days). A goal needs a month, not a day, so a calendar of days is the wrong tool anyway. All options below are clickable.

A · Tidy field, browser popup

Keeps the browser's picker but the field reads in words: "Any time" or "June 2027", with Tallyn's calendar icon. Smallest change; the popup still looks different per browser.

By (optional)

B · Month grid (like Apple Calendar's month view)

The field opens a small Tallyn panel: the year with arrows, then 12 months. Past months are greyed out, this month has a ring. "No date" clears it. Same look in every browser, big targets for thumbs.

By (optional)

C · Quick picks (like Google's "remind me" chips)

Most goals are "in about a year". Chips set it in one tap; "Pick a month" shows two plain menus (month, year).

By (optional)

No date: Tallyn won't say how much to put aside each month.

Mix (recommended)

Field from A (reads in words, and adds how far away it is: "June 2027 · 8 months away"). Panel from B (year arrows and the month grid, same in every browser). Chips from C on top of the grid, for the one-tap "1 year". The grid shows which month a chip picked, so nothing is hidden.
