Plan from your income: new charts

Five chart types Tallyn doesn't use yet, each tried for the plan, then a mix that uses each where it works best. Setting it up stays as in draft 1.

Option A: Waffle: every dollar a square

Your income as 100 squares, one per $64, coloured by job. Solid squares are used. It says "every dollar has a job" more literally than any other chart.

Option B: Flow: where the money goes

Income on the left flows out to its five jobs (a Sankey). Band width is the plan; the bar at the end fills as the month goes. Best at showing the split as a whole.

Each band is a job for part of your income. The bar on the right fills as the month goes.

Tallyn can only see what reaches your bank. Retirement taken from your pay first, like a 401(k), isn't counted here.

Option C: Bullet bars: on track today?

A row per job: the pale band is the plan, the bar is what's used, and a tick marks where an even month would be today. The most exact answer to "am I on track?", in the least room.

Option D: Treemap: jobs by size

One block split into tiles sized by each job's share; colour rises inside a tile as it's used. Strong sense of scale (Essentials is over half).

From your income down, step by step, to what hasn't been used yet. Good for "how much is still free this month?"; less good for each job's progress.

Option F: Mix (recommended): B's flow + C's bullets

The flow (B) as the summary: where your income goes, at a glance. Then bullet bars (C) for the detail, because their today-tick answers "on track?" better than anything else. Two charts, two different questions.
