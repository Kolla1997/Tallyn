Brand and design system v1.0 · Sage & Clay

01 Brand

Your money, your family, everything adds up.

Tallyn is a family finance app. Connect bank accounts in any currency, track spending and savings together, and open joint accounts for the people you love. The name joins tally (counting) and kin (family).

Personality

Warm · Trustworthy · Modern · Family-friendly · Smart · Simple · Premium but approachable.

Tagline

It all adds up.

Use sparingly, in lockups, splash screens and closing sections.

The mark

A person and a sprout in one shape: a dot above two leaves. Sage and clay are the two halves of a family; the dot is the person at the centre.

02 Logo

Logo system

The wordmark is set in Jedira, supplied as outlines only. Never retype it. The tagline is Montserrat Medium.

Versions

Colour on cream or white · Reversed on cocoa · Mono cocoa · Mono cream (use on clay, sage and photos). Horizontal is the default; stacked for square spaces; the mark alone for app icons, favicons and avatars.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the width of the dot on every side. Minimum widths: mark 16px, horizontal lockup 96px (print 24mm), stacked lockup 72px. Below that, use the mark only.
