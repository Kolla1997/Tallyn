# Brand Guide

## Positioning
**For** families who share money across people, accounts and currencies, **Tallyn is** a family finance app **that** shows everything in one place and lets everyone have their own role. **Unlike** ___, **we** ___.

## Name and tagline
- Name: **Tallyn**. Capital T, never all-caps in body text.
- Tagline: **It all adds up.**

- Name story: > [!todo] e.g. from "tally": counting it all up

## Personality
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Calm and trustworthy | Flashy or salesy |
| Clear | Jargon-heavy |

## Communications checklist
Before anything goes out (site, social, deck, email, app store):
- [ ] Uses an approved logo variant with correct clear space
- [ ] Colors come from `design-tokens.json` only
- [ ] Copy matches [[Voice and Tone]]
- [ ] Product claims match [[Vision]]. No promises about returns or security beyond what's true
- [ ] No real customer financial data in screenshots
