# Voice and Tone

Tallyn is a money app for families. Be clear and calm, never pushy.

## We are
* Clear and calm about money
* Warm, never cute or childish
* Precise with numbers and currencies

## Sample copy
| Where | Copy |
|---|---|
| Hero | All your family's money, in every currency. |
| Onboarding | Family money, in every currency. Link accounts, track spending together and open joint accounts for the people you love. |
| Total | Total balance across all accounts |
| Limit | Leo can spend up to $50 a week |
| Rate | Converted at today's rate: 1 EUR = 1.08 USD |
| Closing | It all adds up. |

> [!warning] Placeholder claims
> "Real-time" tracking and live currency conversion are draft copy. Confirm they match what the product does before launch.

## Numbers
* Show currency symbols and two decimals: $4,210.00, €3,970.00, £1,150.00.
* Positive amounts use Success; negative amounts use Error. Never use Clay for money states.
