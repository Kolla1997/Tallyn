# Vision

## One-liner
**Tallyn is a family finance app. It all adds up.**

## What Tallyn does
Families connect bank accounts and cards in any currency, see one total balance in their home currency, track spending and savings in real time, and open joint accounts for partners, kids and parents.

## Who it's for
| Persona | Need | Today's workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Couples / partners | Shared view of money, shared budgets | > [!todo] |
| Parents with kids | Give kids accounts with spending limits | > [!todo] |
| Adult children + parents | Help manage or watch over a parent's finances | > [!todo] |
| Multi-currency families | One total across countries and currencies | > [!todo] |

## v1 features
1. Connect bank accounts and cards in any currency (Plaid)
2. One total balance, converted to the home currency
3. Joint accounts with member roles and spending limits
4. Shared budgets and spending by category
5. Savings goals in any currency

## Future
- AI assistant over the family's financial data (RAG with pgvector)
