# Vision

## One-liner
**Tallyn is a family finance app. It all adds up.**

## What Tallyn does
Families connect their bank accounts and cards, see one total balance, track spending and savings in real time, and open joint accounts for partners, kids and parents.

> [!warning] US first (Dinesh, 2026-10-30)
> For now Coinroof is US only: US banks, in US dollars. Multi-currency (one total across currencies, converted to the home currency) comes later; see [#11](https://github.com/Kolla1997/Tallyn/issues/11).

## Who it's for
| Persona | Need | Today's workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Couples / partners | Shared view of money, shared budgets | > [!todo] |
| Parents with kids | Give kids accounts with spending limits | > [!todo] |
| Adult children + parents | Help manage or watch over a parent's finances | > [!todo] |
| Multi-currency families (later, #11) | One total across countries and currencies | > [!todo] |

## v1 features
1. Connect US bank accounts and cards (Plaid)
2. One total balance
3. Joint accounts with member roles and spending limits
4. Shared budgets and spending by category
5. Savings goals

## Future
- Multi-currency: accounts in any currency, one total in the home currency (#11)
- AI assistant over the family's financial data (RAG with pgvector)
