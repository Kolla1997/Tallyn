## 1. What Tallyn is

Tallyn helps families see their money in one place. You connect your bank accounts, see your balances and transactions, and choose what to share with the people in your household.

**Tallyn only reads your accounts.** It can't move money, pay bills, transfer funds or stop a payment. Any limits or alerts in Tallyn are reminders only; they don't stop a purchase.

**Tallyn isn't financial, tax or legal advice.** Totals, categories and summaries are there to help you see your money. Make your own decisions, or talk to a qualified professional.

## 2. Who can use Tallyn

- You must be **18 or older** to create an account or a household.
- **Teens aged 13 to 17** can join only when a household owner who is their parent or legal guardian invites them, and only with that parent's or guardian's permission.
- **Children under 13 can't have an account or be invited.** We may change this in the future; we'll update these terms first.
- Tallyn is currently available in the **United States** only.

## 4. Connecting your banks

Tallyn connects to your banks through **Plaid Inc.** ("Plaid"). When you link a bank, you authorise Tallyn and Plaid to read your account information, such as balances and transactions, and you agree to Plaid's handling of your data under the [Plaid End User Privacy Policy](https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy).

Data comes from your bank through Plaid. It may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Always check important figures with your bank. You can unlink a bank at any time; Tallyn then removes that bank's accounts and transactions.

## 5. Households and sharing

- A household has one or more **owners**. Owners can invite people, change their roles and remove them. Important changes ask for a fresh code from your authenticator.
- **Nobody sees your money until you share it.** Each person chooses what they share (balances, transactions, and the time and place of purchases) with each other member, and can change it at any time.
- **Owners and adults automatically see the money of teens in their household.** Teens see only their own.
- **Viewers** see only what people share with them.
- Leaving or being removed from a household ends your access to it. Your own banks stay yours.
